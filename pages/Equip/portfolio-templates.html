Launch Your Portfolio

You've got the template — now let's get it live. Here's everything you need to know about domains, hosting, and launching your portfolio site.

01

Get a Domain

Your domain is your professional identity online. Think of it as your digital storefront address.

Where to buy: Namecheap, Google Domains, Cloudflare
Price range: $10–$15/year
Pro tip: Use your name or brand (e.g., yourname.com)
✦ .com is king ✦ .art is trendy ✦ Keep it short
02

Choose Hosting

Hosting is where your website lives. Good hosting = fast loading times = happy visitors.

Best for artists: Netlify (Free), Vercel (Free), GitHub Pages (Free)
Paid options: SiteGround, Bluehost, Kinsta
Pro tip: Start with a free host — upgrade later
✦ Netlify is artist-friendly ✦ Vercel for devs ✦ Free SSL included
03

Deploy Your Site

Upload your template files and make your portfolio live for the world to see.

Netlify: Drag & drop your folder, done!
GitHub Pages: Push to repo, enable Pages
Pro tip: Connect your domain for a custom URL
✦ Netlify is easiest ✦ GitHub Pages is free ✦ Test before sharing
