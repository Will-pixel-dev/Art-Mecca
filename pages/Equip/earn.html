Get paid to create custom artwork for clients. One of the most direct ways to earn money as an artist.

Best For Portrait artists, illustrators

PRICING GUIDE

How to Price Your Art

Pricing your art can be challenging. Here's a framework to help you value your work fairly.

Hourly Rate

Calculate your desired hourly rate × hours worked.

Example: $50/hour × 10 hours = $500

Size-Based

Price per square inch or centimeter.

Example: $5/sq inch × 100 sq inches = $500

Value-Based

Price based on the value to the client.

Example: Commercial use = higher price

Market Research

See what similar artists are charging.

Example: Research competitors' prices

💡 Pricing Tips

  • Don't undervalue your work
  • Factor in materials, time, and overhead
  • Raise your prices as your skills grow
  • Be transparent with clients about pricing

TAX TIPS

Tax Tips for Artists

Don't let taxes scare you — here's what every artist needs to know.

Track Everything

Keep receipts for all art supplies, software, and equipment.

Home Office Deduction

If you work from home, you may be able to deduct a portion of your rent/mortgage.

Set Aside 30%

Put aside 30% of your income for taxes — you'll thank yourself later.

Consult a Pro

Find an accountant who specializes in creative professionals.
