Interview with Mira Chen: Finding Your Creative Voice

Art Mecca Team January 15, 2026 8 min read
🎨

Mira Chen is a digital artist and illustrator based in Berlin. Her work explores the intersection of technology and nature, creating surreal landscapes that feel both familiar and otherworldly. We sat down with Mira to talk about her journey, her creative process, and the lessons she's learned along the way.

From Hobby to Career

Mira's journey into professional art wasn't a straight line. "I started drawing as a hobby when I was a kid," she says. "It wasn't until I was in my early twenties that I realized I could actually make a living from it."

Like many artists, Mira faced doubts and uncertainty. "There was a moment when I had to decide whether to pursue art seriously or keep it as a side thing. I chose to take the leap, and it was the best decision I ever made."

Finding Your Voice

When asked about developing her unique style, Mira emphasizes the importance of experimentation. "Your voice doesn't appear overnight. It's something you discover through making a lot of work — some of it good, some of it bad."

"Don't be afraid to make bad art. Every piece you create teaches you something. The bad ones are just as valuable as the good ones."

— Mira Chen

Overcoming Challenges

Mira is candid about the challenges she's faced. "Imposter syndrome is real. There are days when I look at my work and wonder if I'm good enough. But I've learned to push through that voice."

Her advice to emerging artists: "Keep going. The only artists who don't reach the success they're looking for are the ones who stop working."

What's Next

Mira is currently working on a new series exploring digital identity and the relationship between humans and technology. "I'm fascinated by how we present ourselves online and how that shapes who we are."
