Interview with Alex Rivera: Building a Creative Business

Art Mecca Team December 20, 2025 7 min read
🖌️

Alex Rivera is a freelance illustrator and creative business coach based in Los Angeles. Over the past decade, he's built a thriving career working with clients ranging from indie game studios to major advertising agencies. We spoke with Alex about the challenges of freelancing and the lessons he's learned building his creative business.

From Passion to Profession

Alex's journey started like many artists': with a love for drawing and a dream of making a living from it. "I never thought it was possible," he says. "I always thought art was something you did on the side, not as a career."

That changed when he started getting commissions through social media. "Suddenly I had more work than I could handle. That's when I realized I could actually do this full-time."

The Challenge of Freelancing

Freelancing comes with unique challenges. "The biggest struggle is the uncertainty," Alex admits. "One month you're booked solid, the next you're wondering where your next paycheck is coming from."

His advice: "Build a financial buffer. Save at least six months of living expenses. It gives you the freedom to say no to bad projects and wait for the right opportunities."

Finding the Right Clients

Alex emphasizes the importance of finding clients who align with your values and work style. "Not all clients are the right fit. Some will drain your energy, others will inspire you."

  • Be selective: You don't have to say yes to every project that comes your way.
  • Ask questions: Before agreeing to a project, make sure you understand the scope, timeline, and budget.
  • Set boundaries: Clear contracts and communication prevent misunderstandings.

"The most important business skill you can develop is the ability to say no. It allows you to say yes to the right opportunities."

— Alex Rivera

Building a Sustainable Career

For Alex, sustainability means not just financial stability, but also creative fulfillment. "I want to be doing this for the next twenty years. That means I need to protect my mental health and creative energy."

His tips for long-term sustainability:

  • Diversify your income: Don't rely on a single source of income. Explore prints, classes, books, and licensing.
  • Invest in yourself: Take courses, attend workshops, and keep learning.
  • Build a network: Connect with other freelancers. They understand your struggles and can offer support and referrals.

What's Next for Alex

Alex is currently working on expanding his coaching business. "I want to help more artists build sustainable careers. The world needs more art, and we need more artists who can thrive."

He's also working on a book about freelancing for creatives. "It's the book I wish I had when I was starting out."
