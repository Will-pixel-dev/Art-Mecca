Welcome to the Art Mecca Support Center. Find answers to common questions and guides to help you get started.

Getting Started

Creating an Account

  • Click "Sign Up" in the top right
  • Enter your email, username, and password
  • Verify your email address

Uploading Artwork

  • Go to your profile and click "Upload"
  • Select an image (PNG, JPG, WebP, max 10MB)
  • Add a title, description, tags, and category

Troubleshooting

Can't Login?

  • Check your email and password
  • Use "Forgot Password" to reset
  • Clear your browser cache

Upload Issues

  • File must be under 10MB
  • Use supported formats: PNG, JPG, WebP
  • Check your internet connection

Still need help? Contact our support team
