How we handle your data

Last updated: July 2026

Art Mecca is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address, username, display name
  • Profile information: bio, avatar, social links

Content

  • Artworks, comments, and messages
  • Usage data: views, likes, cheers

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our services
  • To communicate important updates
  • To detect and prevent abuse

3. Data Storage

  • Data is stored on secure cloud servers
  • Retained while your account is active
  • You can delete your account at any time

4. Your Rights

  • Access, rectify, or delete your data
  • Export your data from settings

4.5 Data Subject Request

You can request:

  • Access — View all data we hold about you
  • Rectification — Correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion — Delete your account and data
  • Export — Download your data (available in settings)

Submit requests to privacy@artmecca.com

5. Children's Privacy

Art Mecca is intended for users 13 years and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use our platform. If we discover a user under 13, their account will be terminated.

6. Contact

Questions? privacy@artmecca.com
