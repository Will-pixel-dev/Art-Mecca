Guidelines

Rules and expectations for the Art Mecca community

✦ RULES ✦
Home/ Admin/ Guidelines

Welcome to Art Mecca. Our community is built on creativity, respect, and mutual support.

Our Mission: To create a space where digital artists of all skill levels can learn, grow, and share their work.

1. Be Respectful

  • Treat every member with kindness and respect
  • Harassment, bullying, or discrimination is not tolerated
  • No personal attacks, threats, or intimidation

2. Share Original Work

  • Post only your own original creations
  • Credit references or sources properly
  • AI-generated images are not tolerated

2.5 Copyright / DMCA Takedown

If you believe your copyrighted work has been used without permission:

  1. Email us at copyright@artmecca.com
  2. Include: Your contact info, the infringing URL, and proof of ownership
  3. We will review and remove infringing content within 48 hours

3. Tag NSFW Content Properly

  • Use the NSFW tag for mature or explicit content
  • Users under 18 cannot view NSFW content
  • All NSFW content must be artistic in nature
  • Failure to tag NSFW content may result in:
    • First offense → Warning + Content removal
    • Second offense → Temporary suspension
    • Third offense → Permanent account ban

4. Moderation Process

  • Report: Users can report violations
  • Review: Moderators review reports within 48 hours
  • Action: Warnings, content removal, or account suspension
  • Appeal: Users can appeal decisions via support@artmecca.com

5. Give Constructive Feedback

  • Start with something positive
  • Be specific and offer actionable suggestions
  • Remember that every artist is at a different stage

Consequences: First offense → Warning • Second → Suspension • Third → Permanent ban

Report violations to support@artmecca.com
