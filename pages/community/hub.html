✦ How It Works

Challenges, Cheers & Prizes

Every day we post a creative prompt. Submit your interpretation. The community cheers for their favorites, and the artwork with the most cheers at the end of each challenge wins. The four Intuit challenges — Daily, Weekly, Monthly, and Yearly — are the ones that qualify for the big prizes and badges.

Daily
Resets every 24 hours
Daily Doodle Badge + featured in the daily spotlight
Weekly
Resets every Monday
Weekly Winner Badge + social media feature
Monthly
Resets on the 1st
Monthly Master Badge + 7-day homepage feature
Yearly · Grand Prize
Full year commitment
Soul Chaser Badge + Featured Artist + Custom Challenge Theme
