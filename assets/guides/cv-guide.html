✦ 01
Introduction — Why Your CV Matters

Your CV is often the first impression you make on galleries, studios, and clients. It's your professional story — a snapshot of your experience, skills, and achievements as an artist.

"A great CV doesn't just list what you've done — it tells a story about who you are as an artist and where you're going."

— Industry Professional

Why Artists Need a Strong CV

Reason Why It Matters
First Impression Often the first thing people see
Professional Credibility Shows you're serious about your practice
Career Opportunities Opens doors to exhibitions, residencies, and jobs
Grants & Funding Required for most applications
Networking Makes it easy for people to understand your work
✦ 02
CV vs Resume — What's the Difference?
CV Resume
Length: 2-4 pages Length: 1 page
Content: Comprehensive Content: Condensed
Focus: Full career history Focus: Relevant experience
Use: Academic, galleries, grants Use: Jobs, commercial work
For Artists: Standard For Artists: Used for studio jobs

Which One Should You Use?

  • Use a CV when applying to galleries, residencies, grants, or academic positions
  • Use a Resume when applying for studio jobs, commercial work, or freelance positions
💡

Pro Tip: Many artists have both — a full CV for galleries and a condensed 1-page resume for quick applications.

Section What to Include

Education Degrees, institutions, years attended

Section What to Include

How to Structure — Organize for Impact

The Professional Artist CV Structure

CONTACT INFORMATION
└── Name, email, phone, website, location
ARTIST STATEMENT (Optional)
└── 2-3 sentences on your practice
EDUCATION
└── Degrees, institutions, years
SOLO EXHIBITIONS
└── Show title, venue, year
GROUP EXHIBITIONS
└── Show title, venue, year
COMMISSIONS / PROJECTS
└── Project name, client, year
AWARDS & GRANTS
└── Award name, institution, year
PUBLICATIONS & PRESS
└── Publication name, feature, date
TEACHING & LECTURES
└── Institution, course, year
SKILLS
└── Technical skills, media, software
REFERENCES
└── Name, title, contact info
💡

Pro Tip: Always list sections in reverse chronological order (most recent first). Galleries want to see what you've done recently.

✦ 05
Writing Tips — Make It Stand Out
✓ DO
  • Be specific — "Curated group show featuring 15 artists from 6 countries"
  • Use action words — "Created, developed, curated, led, directed"
  • Quantify achievements — "Exhibited in 12 shows across 4 countries"
  • Tailor to audience — Research what the gallery/studio values
  • Proofread — Typos = unprofessional
✗ DON'T
  • Be vague — "Exhibited internationally" → be specific about where
  • Include everything — Only include relevant experience
  • Use clichés — "Passionate artist" is overused
  • Forget contact info — Make it easy to reach you
  • Ignore formatting — Looks matter

Example: Good vs Great

✓ GOOD

"I had a show at a gallery in New York."

✦ BETTER

"Solo Exhibition: 'Neon Dreams' at Chelsea Gallery, New York — March 2025. Featured 15 works exploring digital identity."

Industry Expectations — What Galleries & Studios Want

What Galleries Look For

Factor Why It Matters
Consistency Shows you're a working artist
Quality over quantity Fewer, better exhibitions
Relevance Work fits their aesthetic
Professionalism Shows you're easy to work with
Career trajectory Shows growth and commitment

What Studios Look For

Factor Why It Matters
Skills Can you do the job?
Experience Have you done similar work?
Portfolio Is your work quality?
Personality Will you fit in?
Professionalism Are you reliable?

Red Flags

✗ Typos and grammatical errors
✗ Outdated information
✗ Unprofessional email address (e.g., coolartist123@gmail.com)
✗ Too much personal information
✗ Missing contact info
💡

Pro Tip: Have someone else review your CV before sending it. Fresh eyes catch mistakes you'll miss.

Portfolio Integration — Connect Your CV to Your Work

Essential: Include Your Portfolio Link

Your CV should always include a link to your portfolio. Make it prominent and easy to find.

Website: www.yourportfolio.com

Pro Tip: Link Specific Work

If you're applying for a specific opportunity, mention relevant work:

"For more information, see my portfolio at www.yourportfolio.com — particularly the 'Cyberpunk Series' (2024-2025)."

Launch Checklist — Before You Send It

Content Check

◻ Name is correct and matches your portfolio
◻ Contact info is accurate and professional
◻ All dates are correct
◻ No typos or grammatical errors
◻ Consistent formatting throughout
◻ Portfolio link works and is up-to-date

Design Check

◻ Clean, professional layout
◻ Consistent font usage
◻ Balanced white space
◻ PDF format (not Word)
◻ File name is professional (ArtistName_CV.pdf)

Audience Check

◻ Tailored to the specific opportunity
◻ Highlights relevant experience
◻ Shows your best work
◻ Makes it easy to contact you
